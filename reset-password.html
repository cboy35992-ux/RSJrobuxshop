Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Gamepass order take?

Roblox gamepass proceeds can remain pending before delivery. Your order timeline and admin chat will show progress.

How does Instant stock work?

Instant stock is reserved when your receipt is submitted. The reservation expires if the order cannot proceed in time.

How do I clarify a gifting order?

Submit the exact game and item, then use your private order chat to confirm scheduling and requirements.

Where can I see unread replies?

Your Dashboard shows unread message badges beside each order.
